earwitness

An independent verification layer for conversational phone agents. It rebuilds what actually happened on a call from transcript evidence and never accepts the provider's self-report. The assertion engine is split by dimension: grounded, terminated cleanly, never leaked instructions. 101 test cases across 18 files, all passing.

为电话对话 agent 做的独立核验层。只根据通话记录重建实际发生了什么，从不接受服务商的自报结果。断言引擎按维度拆分：有依据、正常结束、没有泄露指令。18 个文件共 101 个测试用例，全部通过。

second-pass A null result, made to stick

Every answer is grounded in a real ClickHouse query run through an MCP server. The project's own selling point was tested on day one and failed: across three films and 18 tests against Bonferroni-corrected thresholds, one result cleared the floor by a hair and did not replicate. The write-up also publishes my own errors: end credits had been scored as film, which produced the original 4.40× headline (1.28× on the film itself).

每个回答都来自通过 MCP 服务器执行的真实 ClickHouse 查询。项目自己的核心卖点第一天就被测了，结果没站住：三部片、18 项检验按 Bonferroni 校正门槛判，只有一项勉强过线且不能复现。文档也公开了我自己的错误：片尾字幕曾被当成正片打分，造成最初「后半段注意力 4.40 倍」的结论（正片本身是 1.28 倍）。

table-for-two Learned robot policy

An evaluation designed to resist self-deception: 10 evaluation seeds plus 30 never-seen seeds, with a Wilson confidence interval on every success rate. The learned policy scored 0/10 on the full table task, reported with the reasons, against a scripted pipeline at 10/10. INT8 quantisation cut inference from 16.5 ms to 4.9 ms. Submitted to the Intel Physical AI Online Challenge.

为防自欺而设计的评测：10 个评测种子加 30 个从没见过的种子，每个成功率都带 Wilson 置信区间。学习策略在完整任务上 0/10，照实汇报并解释原因，脚本流程为 10/10。INT8 量化把推理从 16.5 ms 降到 4.9 ms。已提交到 Intel Physical AI 线上挑战赛。

playtest-agent Playtest engine

A general-purpose playtest engine where all game-specific knowledge lives in swappable profiles; it has run across six titles and versions. Each known screen maps to one whitelisted action, and an unrecognised static screen is escalated to a human. In a full first-session run it made zero mis-taps on payment buttons.

通用的游戏走查引擎，所有和具体游戏相关的知识都放在可替换的配置里，已在六个游戏和版本上跑过。每个已知界面只对应一个白名单操作，遇到不认识的静止界面就交给人工。完整的首日实测里零误点付费按钮。

AI playtest on a live build在线上版本上做 AI 走查

Drove a live build with my own playtest engine. The first run produced 13 severity-ranked findings with repro evidence. When a recording subsystem silently wrote empty files, I marked the cause unconfirmed and downgraded the two findings that depended on playback.

用自己的走查引擎跑线上版本，第一次就产出 13 条按严重程度分级、带复现证据的问题。录像子系统悄悄写出空文件时，我把原因标为「未确认」，并把依赖录像的两条结论主动降级。

Evidence-graded teardown按证据分级的竞品拆解

Graded every claim by evidence strength and barred unverified claims from conclusions. After joining live payment data I refuted three of my own findings, and signed off 28 conclusions through a line-by-line review ledger.

每条结论都按证据强弱分级，未经验证的不能进结论。接入真实付费数据后推翻了自己的三条结论，28 条结论逐条过评审台账后才签字。
