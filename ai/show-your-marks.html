Compared quality, latency and cost to select the judge. On one synthetic case, 24 adversarial prompts produced 1.33 leaked facts per attack in the full-context control and none in the guarded user-visible output. Per-session and daily spend caps limit model spend.

比较质量、延迟与成本后选择裁判。单个合成病例的 24 次攻击中，全量上下文对照组每次泄露 1.33 个锁定事实，守卫后的用户可见输出未观测到泄露。每局与每日设置模型花费上限。

Exact-line accuracy: the predicted set of rubric items matches the labelled set for a line. Scope: one synthetic case; no clinician review. These results do not establish clinical validity or general safety.精确行准确率：每句话预测的评分项集合与标注集合完全一致的比例。范围：单个合成病例，未经临床审核。上述结果不代表临床有效性或普遍安全性。
