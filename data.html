Methods from live work工作中的方法案例

From a live F2P mobile title (live since May 2026). Studio and game are anonymised; no business figures are shown.

来自一款已上线运营的 F2P 手游（2026 年 5 月上线）。工作室和游戏名已匿名，不展示任何业务数值。

1 · First-month live diagnostic1 · 开服首月数据诊断

Problem问题

After launch, the team needed to know which problems were real and which were noise across many separate reports.

上线后，团队需要从大量分散的报表里分清哪些是真问题、哪些是噪音。

Approach做法

Ingested 11 analytics reports and cross-validated key KPIs through business reconciliation before trusting any of them.

汇总 11 份报表，先用业务勾稽关系交叉校验关键指标，再决定信哪些数据。

What it shows体现的能力

Three systemic issues, each backed by several metrics: uneven acquisition quality, shallow payment depth, and difficulty walls that churn players rather than convert them.

找出三个系统性问题，每个都有多项指标佐证：买量质量参差、付费深度偏浅、关卡卡点导致流失而不是转化。

2 · A/B test design2 · A/B 实验设计

Problem问题

Test a mid-tier monetisation offer without hurting retention or inviting early, biased stops.

测试一个中档商业化礼包，既不能伤留存，也要避免提前看结果带来的偏差。

Approach做法

One primary metric (ARPPU), retention and refund rate as guardrails, sample plan and stop rule fixed in advance, results re-checked by payer segment.

一个主指标（ARPPU），留存和退款率作护栏；样本量和停止规则提前定好；按付费分层复核结果。

What it shows体现的能力

Backed by incremental-revenue scenarios with ±20% sensitivity and an LTV-versus-CPA break-even read on acquisition spend.

配了 ±20% 敏感度的增量收入情景，以及 LTV 对比 CPA 的买量回本判断。

3 · Day-2 retention study3 · 次留专题

Problem问题

Grouping players by their current progress made high-progress groups look sticky by definition: the players who stayed are the ones who progressed.

按玩家「当前进度」分组，高进度组天然显得留存高：留下来的人本来就是进度高的人。

Approach做法

Separated snapshot attributes from day-1 event data and reported only on the latter.

把「当前快照」类字段和「首日事件」数据分开，只用后者做汇报口径。

What it shows体现的能力

First-session progression depth as the strongest day-2 predictor, and the highest-ROI player segment to move.

找出次留最强的预测因素是首日推进深度，并定位了最值得撬动的玩家人群。

4 · Post-launch retrospective4 · 改版上线后复盘

Problem问题

A monetisation rework and an onboarding change shipped together. Did either one actually work, or did traffic change?

商业化改版和新手流程改动同时上线。到底哪个起了作用，还是只是流量变了？

Approach做法

Designed a before/after read that controlled for active-user and acquisition changes, separating design-driven lift from traffic-driven lift.

设计前后对比口径，控制活跃和买量的变化，把「设计带来的提升」和「流量带来的提升」分开。

What it shows体现的能力

An honest null result: payment efficiency improved, but the onboarding change did not move retention, so the next iteration targeted first-session depth instead.

如实汇报无效结果：付费效率提升了，新手流程改动却没有带动留存，所以下一轮改为直接针对首日推进深度。

5 · Metric definitions and repeatable methods5 · 指标体系与方法沉淀

Problem问题

Reports disagreed because the same metric was defined differently in different places.

同一个指标在不同地方定义不同，导致报表对不上。

Approach做法

Wrote a data requirements document with a 38-metric dictionary (locked definitions, formulas and granularity), drill-down dimensions and instrumentation requirements.

撰写数据需求文档：38 个指标的字典（口径、公式、粒度全部锁定），加下钻维度和埋点需求。

What it shows体现的能力

Analytics and engineering built reporting from it. The analysis approach became a reusable four-level template (describe → locate → drive → verify) and a version-update attribution runbook.

数据团队和程序据此搭建报表。分析方法也整理成可复用的四层模板（描述 → 定位 → 驱动 → 验证）和版本更新归因手册。

dbt starter on DuckDB

Public jaffle_shop sample data, modelled in three layers so each question (revenue by order, customer value, monthly retention) is answered by one tested mart. Order sequence and cohort month come from window functions. 56 data tests pass, including three reconciliation checks: revenue in the marts equals raw payments, every order is counted once, and month-zero retention is exactly 100%. Generated docs and a CI build that fails on any test failure.

用 dbt 官方公开样例数据 jaffle_shop，分三层建模，让「每单收入、客户价值、月度留存」三个问题各由一张有测试的 mart 表回答。订单序号和 cohort 月份用窗口函数算出。56 个数据测试全部通过，其中三条是对账测试：mart 里的总收入等于原始支付总额、每单只计一次、首月留存恰好是 100%。附自动生成的文档，CI 里任何测试失败都会让构建失败。

Grouped cross-validation

Rock-type classification from well logs on a public dataset (3,164 rows, 7 wells, 9 classes). Scored twice: shuffled K-fold, and holding out whole wells, which is how the model would really be used. Random K-fold made LightGBM look like a 0.71 macro-F1 model, clearly ahead of logistic regression; held out by well it scores 0.38 and ties it. The leak inflated the score and would have picked the wrong model. 26 tests.

用公开测井数据（3,164 行、7 口井、9 类岩相）做岩相分类，评估两次：随机打乱的 K 折，以及整口井留出（这才是模型真实的用法）。随机 K 折下 LightGBM 的 macro-F1 是 0.71，明显好过逻辑回归；按井留出后只有 0.38，和逻辑回归打平。数据泄漏抬高了分数，还会让人选错模型。26 个测试。

second-pass

Audience attention versus shot content in films, with every answer grounded in a real ClickHouse query. The obvious story did not survive: attention rises toward the end of almost every film regardless of content, so raw correlations are confounded. Across three films and 18 tests against Bonferroni-corrected thresholds, one result cleared the floor by a hair and did not replicate. The write-up also publishes my own errors: end credits had been scored as film, which produced the original 4.40× headline (1.28× on the film itself).

分析电影中观众注意力和镜头内容的关系，每个回答都来自真实的 ClickHouse 查询。最直观的结论没有站住：几乎每部片子后半段的注意力都会上升，和内容无关，所以直接做相关会被这个趋势干扰。三部片、18 项检验按 Bonferroni 校正后的阈值判断，只有一项勉强过线，而且没有复现。文档也公开了我自己的错误：片尾字幕曾被当作正片打分，最初“后半段是前半段 4.40 倍”的结论由此而来（正片本身是 1.28 倍）。
